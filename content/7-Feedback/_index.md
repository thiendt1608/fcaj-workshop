### Overall impressions of the program
First Cloud AI Journey let me build a real project end to end on AWS instead of isolated labs: proposal, serverless architecture, Bedrock, CI/CD, cost measurement and clean-up. Working as a team with issues, PR reviews and ADRs got me used to how a software team operates. The program's rules site states the requirements and the scoring clearly, so it is easy to check my own work against it.

### Satisfaction
**4.5/5.** I learned the most from getting an AI model running on AWS and measuring it with real numbers. Half a point off because some requirements changed close to the deadline.

### What could be improved
* The rules were updated on 21–24/11 (the Step-by-step Lab Guide became mandatory) while the deadline is 29/11, leaving teams little time to adjust. Fixing requirements earlier, or announcing changes with a transition period, would help.
* Getting Bedrock model access and a team AWS account took time early on; a guide or ready accounts from week one would help AI teams avoid delays.
* A sample workshop for a multi-service project (not only a single-service lab) would show interns how much detail is expected.

### Would I recommend it to friends? Why?
**Yes.** It suits anyone who wants to learn cloud and AI by building a real project, with a team workflow and access to AWS services that are hard to try on your own. I would tell them to plan for a significant time commitment.
